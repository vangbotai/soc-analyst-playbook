# Escalation Process

## Purpose

Not every alert requires immediate escalation. The objective is to identify incidents that require additional investigation by senior analysts or incident responders.

## Escalate When

* Malware is detected.
* Administrative accounts are compromised.
* Multiple failed logins indicate a brute-force attack.
* Sensitive data may have been exposed.
* Unauthorized privilege escalation is identified.
* Suspicious PowerShell or command-line activity is observed.
* Lateral movement between systems is suspected.

## Information to Include

Before escalating, document:

* Time of detection
* Systems involved
* Users involved
* Alert severity
* Summary of findings
* Supporting evidence
* Actions already performed

## Communication

Escalations should be:

* Accurate
* Concise
* Objective
* Supported by available evidence

Avoid assumptions and clearly distinguish confirmed facts from observations requiring further investigation.
